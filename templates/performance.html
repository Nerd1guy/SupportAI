1. Preprocessing

Lowercasing, regex URL/email stripping, special character removal, whitespace normalization.

2. TF-IDF Vectors

Unigram + Bigram (1,2) token extraction, sublinear term frequency scaling, 2,500 max features.

3. Classification

Logistic Regression vs Random Forest benchmarked on 80/20 stratified split. Best model deployed.

4. Response Synthesis

Dynamic blending of category actions, priority SLAs, and empathetic tone into customer response.
